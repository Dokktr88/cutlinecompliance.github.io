ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility Statement

Cutline Compliance is committed to making its public digital content usable by as many people as practical, including people who use assistive technology.

Accessibility target

Cutline's public website is developed and reviewed with WCAG 2.2 Level AA as the working accessibility target. Accessibility is treated as an ongoing quality requirement rather than a one-time certification claim.

Current practices

  • Semantic headings and page landmarks.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus indicators.
  • Skip-to-content links.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Responsive layouts for smaller screens and zoomed views.
  • Reduced-motion support for visitors who request it through operating-system or browser settings.
  • Contrast review for text and interactive controls.

Known limits

Some linked third-party services, embedded documents, downloadable files, or external platforms are outside Cutline's direct control. Cutline will continue to improve first-party content and will provide a reasonable alternate format for Cutline-controlled material when feasible.

Report an access problem

If you encounter a barrier, identify the page or file, describe the problem, and include the assistive technology or browser involved if relevant. Send the report to hello@cutlinecompliance.com.
