METHOD

Intelligence is useful only when the evidence survives the conclusion.

Cutline applies a disciplined public-source workflow before publishing substantive analysis.

1. Establish the record

Start with controlling law, regulation, official public material, credible economic evidence, and attributable stakeholder statements.

2. Classify the evidence

Separate verified fact, supported interpretation, uncertainty, Cutline-created frameworks, and recommendations.

3. Challenge the conclusion

Test competing explanations, adverse evidence, implementation constraints, quantitative claims, and likely expert criticism before release.

INDEPENDENCE & SCOPE

Independent analysis. Clear boundaries.

Cutline Intelligence uses publicly available information for educational and analytical purposes. It does not provide establishment-specific regulatory advice to USDA-FSIS regulated establishments and does not represent the views, findings, policy, or official position of USDA, FSIS, or any other government agency.

Substantive analysis is subject to source verification, domain review, and final editorial approval by Cutline Compliance.
