---

## 1. Purpose

This document preserves the next major website upgrade so Cutline Compliance can return to it later without reopening design strategy from scratch.

The current production website is considered complete for the present stage of the business. Website v3.0 is **not an immediate redesign**. It is a future intelligence-experience upgrade intended for a point when increased traffic, published work, Academy activity, and market validation justify additional investment.

The objective of v3.0 is to move two remaining perception categories from strong to exceptional:

- **Immediate visual impact:** target 10/10
- **Intelligence-company feel:** target 10/10

The approach is to keep the professional light corporate shell established in v2.3 and add a proprietary intelligence layer rather than returning to an all-dark interface.

---

## 2. Core Build Thesis

**Dark mode made Cutline look technological. Website v3 should make Cutline look intelligent.**

The future site should not merely state that Cutline performs intelligence and systems analysis. It should visually demonstrate the process of moving from signals → patterns → interpretation → decision-relevant understanding.

The visual experience should feel closer to a serious research, intelligence, and strategy organization than to a generic SaaS dashboard.

## 4. Activation Gate

Do not begin Website v3 simply because the design is possible. Begin when the site has enough real activity and evidence to justify a richer presentation.

### Recommended activation indicators

Website v3 should be considered when several of the following are true:

- Website traffic shows a sustained upward trend over multiple months.
- Cutline has multiple published intelligence products or research assets worth surfacing as a library rather than a single publication.
- Cutline Academy has active learners, course completions, or additional programs beyond the initial flagship.
- The Compliance Readiness Diagnostic has enough pilot use or market feedback to support stronger product proof.
- Visitors are regularly reaching product, intelligence, Academy, or publication pages from search, LinkedIn, referrals, or direct traffic.
- Existing analytics show that improving engagement, publication discovery, or conversion is likely to produce measurable value.
- The current homepage has become a traffic destination rather than primarily a digital business card.

### Activation decision

Traffic alone is not sufficient. The upgrade should be justified by a combination of **audience + content + proof + business value**.

## 5. Website v3 Experience Architecture

### Module A — Signature Cutline Signal Map

Replace the current static hero framework panel with a proprietary intelligence visualization while preserving the light corporate hero shell.

**Concept:**

`Operational signals → People / Processes / Product → Pattern convergence → Readiness / Intelligence → Decision-relevant understanding`

The visualization should include:

- People, Processes, and Product signal nodes
- signal relationships or connection lines
- pattern convergence
- evidence/confidence states where appropriate
- a central Cutline interpretation layer
- a visible visual “cutline” separating raw observations from interpreted intelligence
- restrained animation on capable devices
- a fully static accessible fallback

**Design intent:**

The hero should communicate the Cutline thesis before a visitor reads a paragraph.

**Avoid:**

- fake real-time data
- fictional dashboards
- flashing alerts
- cybersecurity aesthetics
- unnecessary 3D effects
- dense animations
- decorative data with no conceptual meaning

---

### Module B — Current Intelligence Feature

Immediately below the hero, create a prominent editorial module for current Cutline intelligence.

Structure:

- `CURRENT INTELLIGENCE` label
- publication title
- publication date
- one verified key finding or central analytical question
- publication category
- read-analysis action
- download action when applicable
- optional source/evidence count only when accurately supportable

Supporting cards may surface:

- Cutline Academy
- Applied Readiness Continuum
- Compliance Readiness Diagnostic

**Purpose:** demonstrate substantive work before asking visitors to accept positioning claims.

---

### Module C — Cutline Intelligence Visual Grammar

Develop a reusable proprietary visual language that appears across the website, intelligence reports, presentations, and future publications.

Candidate components:

- signal nodes
- convergence lines
- evidence bands
- confidence markers
- verification tags
- interpretation tags
- assessment labels
- source classes
- timeline markers
- pattern maps
- system-pressure indicators
- decision-impact markers

The system should visually distinguish:

- Verified Fact / Evidence
- Supported Interpretation
- Assumption
- Cutline-Created Framework
- Recommendation

This should become recognizable as Cutline even when the logo is absent.

---

### Module D — Editorial Research Presentation

Reduce dependence on repeated generic card layouts.

Introduce selective editorial elements such as:

- oversized quantitative findings
- pull findings
- evidence bands
- section numbers
- annotated diagrams
- publication covers
- clean charts
- timelines
- systems maps
- source/methodology panels

**Rule:** information graphics must carry meaning. Decorative data visualization is prohibited.

---

### Module E — Evidence / Proof Moment

Create one prominent evidence block on the homepage using only verifiable Cutline work.

Potential examples when the underlying evidence exists:

- states analyzed
- public sources reviewed
- pathways mapped
- courses available
- Academy completions
- diagnostic reports produced
- intelligence publications released
- research categories covered

No vanity metrics, invented client counts, fabricated testimonials, or unsupported authority claims.

---

### Module F — Controlled Micro-Motion

Use subtle motion to explain relationships, not to decorate the page.

Potential interactions:

- signal paths activate once on hero load
- evidence markers fade in sequentially
- ARC reveals Learn → Diagnose → Interpret
- publication metadata enters with restrained motion
- small hover states reveal supporting context

Target animation window: approximately 0.8–1.2 seconds for primary entry sequences.

Requirements:

- respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- no looping motion unless informationally necessary
- no parallax required
- no bouncing, spinning, pulsing, or gamified counters

---

### Module G — Stronger Editorial Typography

Retain the approved Aptos-first stack but increase hierarchy through layout rather than a new typeface.

Potential treatment:

- larger desktop hero headline
- tighter headline line-height
- small uppercase intelligence labels
- larger quantitative findings
- stronger publication-title treatment
- more deliberate spacing between analytical sections

Typography must remain readable and professional rather than fashion-driven.

---

### Module H — Distinct Intelligence Section Identity

Cutline Intelligence should feel analytically denser than Academy while remaining within the same brand system.

Possible additions:

- evidence classification
- source counts
- methodology indicators
- confidence language
- timelines
- policy maps
- systems diagrams
- research categories
- intelligence index navigation

The distinction should come from information architecture and analytical depth, not an unrelated color palette.

---

## 7. Intelligence Visualization Design Principles

Every intelligence visual should pass these questions:

1. Does the visual explain a real concept?
2. Can the user understand what the nodes, lines, states, or labels represent?
3. Does it distinguish evidence from interpretation?
4. Does it improve understanding faster than plain text would?
5. Does it remain truthful if a visitor assumes the visualization is substantive?
6. Is the information understandable without animation?
7. Does it look like Cutline rather than a generic SaaS dashboard?

If the answer to any of these is no, simplify or remove the visual.

## 10. Performance Guardrails

The intelligence upgrade must not make the site feel slow or heavy.

Targets should be set using current performance data at build time, but design rules include:

- SVG/CSS/canvas preferred over large videos for the hero intelligence visual
- no autoplay background video
- lazy-load below-the-fold analytical graphics
- avoid heavyweight visualization libraries unless real functionality requires them
- progressive enhancement for animation
- mobile-first fallbacks
- keep downloadable research assets separate from page-rendering assets

A visual-impact gain that materially harms usability is not a successful upgrade.

---

## 11. Ethics and Evidence Guardrails

Website v3 must not use visual sophistication to imply authority or evidence that does not exist.

Required:

- public-source information only where applicable
- no implication of USDA/FSIS endorsement
- no use of nonpublic agency information
- no facility-specific consulting positioning for USDA-FSIS-regulated establishments
- no invented customers, case studies, usage counts, testimonials, certifications, partners, or market rankings
- no simulated intelligence feeds presented as real
- no unsupported “first,” “only,” or market-leadership claims

Visual intelligence must remain analytically honest.

---

## 12. Success Criteria

Website v3 is successful when a first-time qualified visitor can answer within seconds:

- What is Cutline?
- What does Cutline actually produce?
- What makes its approach different?
- How do Academy, CRF, CRD, and LIB relate?
- Why is Cutline using the word intelligence?
- Where can I see evidence of its work?
- What should I click next?

Perception targets:

- Immediate visual impact: 10/10
- Intelligence-company feel: 10/10
- Professional credibility: remain 10/10
- Brand alignment: remain 10/10
- Readability/accessibility: no regression
- Ethics clarity: no regression
- Page performance: no material regression
