PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Effective September 12, 2026. This notice describes privacy practices for the public Cutline Compliance website and public intelligence pages.

What this notice covers

This notice applies to public pages on cutlinecompliance.com. Cutline products hosted on separate application interfaces, including Academy or diagnostic platforms, may provide additional privacy notices for account, payment, enrollment, assessment, or product-use data.

Optional website analytics

Cutline uses Google Analytics only after a visitor selects “Allow analytics.” If analytics is not allowed, the public site continues to function without loading the Google Analytics library.

The site stores the visitor's analytics choice in local browser storage under a Cutline-specific preference key so the choice can be remembered on that browser. Visitors can clear browser site data to reset the choice.

Information you choose to send

If you contact Cutline by email or another linked communication channel, Cutline receives the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, message, and any materials you intentionally attach. Do not submit confidential establishment records or prohibited facility-specific regulatory requests through public contact channels.

External services and links

Public pages may link to services operated by other organizations, including LinkedIn, YouTube, Google, government sources, and product platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices and settings.

How information is used

  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Understand permitted aggregate website usage when analytics is enabled.
  • Improve Cutline content, products, navigation, and user experience.
  • Protect the integrity and security of Cutline websites and products.

Children's privacy

The public Cutline website and professional products are not directed to children under 13. Cutline does not intentionally design the public site to collect information from children.

Changes to this notice

Cutline may update this notice as the website, analytics configuration, or product ecosystem changes. The effective date above will be updated when the notice materially changes.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to hello@cutlinecompliance.com.
