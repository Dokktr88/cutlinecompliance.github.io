ELIGIBILITY BOUNDARY

Facility-specific regulatory consulting is not available to establishments currently regulated by USDA-FSIS.

Cutline Compliance does not provide facility-specific consulting, regulatory strategy, corrective-action guidance, enforcement-response support, representation, or regulatory interpretation to establishments currently regulated by USDA-FSIS.

Permitted educational scope

General public-source education, professional training, systems concepts, and product information may be available where the product's stated terms and intended use allow it.

Prohibited facility-specific scope

Cutline does not review establishment-specific noncompliance matters, enforcement correspondence, corrective actions, appeals, or regulatory strategy for USDA-FSIS regulated establishments.

Ask before submitting

Questions about eligibility should be raised before purchase or before sending operational information. Do not submit confidential or sensitive establishment materials for prohibited review.

RESPONSIBLE USE

Cutline increases understanding; it does not create false certainty.

A course, diagnostic tool, report, article, framework, or website cannot replace legal counsel, qualified professional judgment, official regulatory communication, inspection activity, audit activity, certification review, or other facility-specific review where those are required.

Cutline's role is to help eligible users think more clearly about systems: how work happens, how authority is assigned, how signals are detected, how records reflect reality, and how product protection depends on the connection between People, Processes, and Product.

Core boundary

  • Cutline teaches systems thinking and readiness.
  • Cutline supports education and internal discussion.
  • Cutline may surface patterns and pressure signals within stated product limits.
  • Cutline does not issue approvals, certifications, or regulatory findings.
  • Cutline does not replace qualified professional judgment.

PRODUCT-SPECIFIC SCOPE

Different Cutline products do different jobs.

Scope is defined by the product. Using one Cutline product does not expand the permitted scope of another.

Cutline Academy

Compliance Readiness Diagnostic

Cutline Intelligence

CRD BOUNDARIES

CRD is a system-pattern visibility tool, not a compliance score.

The Compliance Readiness Diagnostic is designed for educational and internal readiness purposes. It helps eligible users identify signals and patterns in how an operation may behave under pressure.

CRD output should be treated as structured visibility for internal discussion. It is not a final determination, not validated evidence of compliance, and not a substitute for professional judgment.

Appropriate useInternal readiness discussion, leadership reflection, training planning, and system-pattern awareness within the user's eligibility and product scope.
Not appropriate useClaiming approval, certification, regulatory compliance, audit success, or inspection readiness based solely on CRD output.
Interpretation standardCRD reports should be read as system behavior signals, not as official conclusions.

CRD does not:

Score compliance

CRD does not assign a compliance score, grade, approval, pass/fail result, or regulatory conclusion.

Interpret law or inspection findings

CRD does not interpret laws, rules, regulations, inspection findings, audit standards, or enforcement actions.

Provide corrective direction

CRD does not provide corrective-action instructions, enforcement strategy, representation, or prohibited facility-specific regulatory guidance.

ETHICAL OPERATING PRINCIPLES

The standard applies to every Cutline product.

  1. Education before persuasion.Public-facing content should create useful understanding rather than rely on fear, authority signaling, or exaggerated claims.
  2. Evidence before certainty.Verified facts, supported interpretation, assumptions, and company-created frameworks should not be blurred together.
  3. Systems before shortcuts.Cutline does not promote binder theater, template dependency, or cosmetic compliance thinking as substitutes for functioning systems.
  4. Boundaries before sales.If a request falls outside the permitted scope, the commercial opportunity does not override the boundary.
  5. Independent identity.Cutline's work is the company's independent educational and analytical work. It is not an agency position, decision, endorsement, or representation.

PUBLIC-SOURCE INTELLIGENCE STANDARD

Show the evidence and show the limits.

Cutline Intelligence relies on public, citable information. Where Cutline creates a framework, classification, model, or recommendation, it should be identified as company-created rather than presented as external validation or official policy.

When evidence is incomplete or competing explanations remain plausible, the analysis should say so. Confidence is earned through transparent sourcing and reasoning, not through stronger wording.

Evidence discipline

  • Verified fact or evidence
  • Supported interpretation
  • Assumption or unresolved question
  • Company-created framework
  • Recommendation
