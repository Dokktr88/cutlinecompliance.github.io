DIAGNOSTIC PRODUCT · LIVE PILOT

Compliance Readiness Diagnostic

CRD is a secure system-pattern visibility tool for eligible users. It helps surface pressure signals, documentation-reality gaps, decision-authority friction, feedback weaknesses, and recurring management-control patterns.

CRD is not a compliance score. It does not make regulatory findings, interpret law, provide corrective-action direction, replace audits or inspections, or create a facility-specific consulting relationship.

Validation status: CRD remains available as a controlled live pilot while Cutline validates market fit, user experience, and commercial positioning. The product may evolve as evidence and user feedback are collected.

Decision AuthorityWho can decide, stop, escalate, or act.
Feedback & DetectionHow signals are noticed and acted on.
Management ControlHow leadership, pressure, and resources shape system behavior.
Documentation LogicHow records, expectations, and operating reality align.

FUTURE INTERPRETATION PRODUCT

Leadership Intelligence Brief

LIB is the planned leadership-facing interpretation layer within the readiness system. Its purpose is to organize observed patterns, pressure signals, confidence, and system implications into a concise decision-oriented briefing for eligible use cases.

LIB is not positioned as an official regulatory interpretation or compliance determination. Release details will be published only when the product scope, evidence standard, and delivery model are ready.

Product discipline

  • Separate verified evidence from interpretation
  • Show confidence and limitations
  • Surface alternative explanations where material
  • Focus leadership attention on system implications
  • Avoid false precision or official-sounding conclusions
